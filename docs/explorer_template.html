04 / THE CONGESTION ZONE

Separate the fee from the economics.

Compare the same calendar months in 2024 and 2025, excluding January. “Zone-linked” means either endpoint is in the data-inferred zone.

A rider charge, under the rule

From January 5, 2025, the CBD toll adds $1.50 per Uber/Lyft passenger trip in, out of, within, or through the zone. This charge is separate from base fare and the older congestion surcharge.

A descriptive comparison

Changes in fare, driver pay, gross take, and demand do not identify who ultimately bears the economic cost. Pricing changes, minimum-pay rules, seasonality, and changing trip mix also matter.

How the zone is inferred

Monthly average speed, calculated as total miles ÷ total trip hours. Zone-linked and other trips can differ in their routes and mix.

06 / READ THE NUMBERS WITH CARE

Definitions, coverage, and limits.

Public records, processed monthly

A consistent trip filter

Keep base fare > $0, driver pay ≥ $0, distance > 0 and ≤ 100 miles, duration 1–240 minutes, and a pickup month matching the file. Wait averages use only waits from 0 to 60 minutes.

Gross revenue is not profit

Dollar values are nominal. Take excludes operating costs; rewards and rider discounts may not be fully captured. Results describe recorded trips, not total driver earnings or causal policy effects. Platform shares cover Uber and Lyft only.
